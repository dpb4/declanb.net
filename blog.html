About Me

Hello. I am Declan, a computer science undergraduate student at the University of British Columbia. I love computers and math, and I like physics/philosophy as well.

My main interests within CS are in graphics, programming languages / compilers, and type theory. That being said, I am interested in most fields of CS, and I'm always open to learning new things. I have been programming since I was young and it's both my hobby and passion. My favourite programming language is Rust.

Besides academics I have many other hobbies. I like video games, reading, cross stitch, and origami, to name a few. I'm not a huge fan of the outdoors, though I can appreciate it in moderation.

If you would like to get in contact, please don't hesitate to reach out. See my other links on the left, or feel free to just send me an email.
